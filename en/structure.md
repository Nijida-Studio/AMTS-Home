## Core model

1. **Specification** – the documented AMTS rules.
2. **AMTS Space** – a concrete knowledge space organized according to AMTS.
3. **Project workspace** – a project's current context, decisions, and handovers.
4. **Archive** – immutable historical session and conversation records.

Archives preserve history but do not replace maintained project knowledge. A repository may contain its own instructions such as `AGENTS.md` and project documentation; an AMTS Space respects these as repository-owned sources.

Example structures are available in [`templates/project`](https://github.com/Nijida-Studio/AMTS-Home/tree/main/templates/project) and [`examples/minimal-space`](https://github.com/Nijida-Studio/AMTS-Home/tree/main/examples/minimal-space).
