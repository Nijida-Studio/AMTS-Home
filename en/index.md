Akari AMTS helps projects preserve useful working knowledge across individual conversations, tools, and assistant environments. The project owns the knowledge; conversations are temporary working sessions.

> AMTS is a specification. It is not an application, framework, runtime, or specific assistant implementation.

The maintained source is available in [AMTS-Home on the `main` branch](https://github.com/Nijida-Studio/AMTS-Home/tree/main) under the Apache License 2.0.
